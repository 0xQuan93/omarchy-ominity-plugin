# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-19

- Replaced the pip illustrations with 78 original symbolic tableaux. Each card now has a larger art window, layered visual depth, and a specific observation on its reading side.
- Redrew the card back as a celestial observatory and enlarged the on-screen card while keeping the clean Noto lettering and smooth turn and close motion.
- Kept the full deck theme-aware in dark and light Omarchy palettes. Art source changes now invalidate the local theme cache so updated illustrations appear immediately.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-19

- Redrew the 78 original cards with larger scenes, more distinct suit compositions, stronger symbols, and crisp Noto Serif/Sans lettering.
- Rendered SVG faces at the active display scale, with extra resolution for fractional scaling. Retained live dark and light theme palettes and readable contrast.
- Replaced the translucent draw crossfade with an opaque card-back-to-art turn. Refined the art-to-reading flip, card edge, entrance, and close motion.
- Enlarged and sharpened the in-card reading typography. The optional Zephyr adapter and daily draw behavior remain compatible.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-19

- Introduced theme-aware cached card art and the card-first flip reading.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-19

- Added the original 78-card daily tarot deck, bar entry, desktop widget, and optional local summary adapter interface.
